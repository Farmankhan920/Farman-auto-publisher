Technical support

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • What you were trying to do and what happened.
  • The date and time (with time zone) of the issue.
  • The video file name or caption involved, if relevant.
  • Any error message you saw (remove private details).

Many publishing issues come from TikTok or Google permissions, quotas or expired authorization. Reconnecting your account often resolves them.
