Data Deletion

You can disconnect Farman Auto Publisher from your accounts and ask us to delete the data we hold. This page explains how.

1. Request account disconnection

You can stop Farman Auto Publisher from accessing your accounts at any time, without needing our help:

Disconnect TikTok

  1. Open the TikTok app and go to Settings and privacy.
  2. Open Security & permissions (the exact name may vary by version) and choose Apps and services or Manage app permissions.
  3. Select Farman Auto Publisher and choose to remove or revoke access.

Disconnect Google

  1. Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Select Farman Auto Publisher (or the related Apps Script project) and choose Remove access.

You may also email us at your-email@example.com asking us to disconnect your account and revoke stored tokens. Revoking access stops any further publishing from taking place.

2. Request deletion of stored data

To ask us to delete data stored about you:

  1. Send an email to your-email@example.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your TikTok username (and, if relevant, the Google account email used with the Service) so we can find your records. Please do not send passwords or tokens.
  3. Tell us whether you want everything deleted or only certain items.
  4. We may reply to verify that the request comes from the account owner.

3. What information may be deleted

TikTok access token and refresh tokenYes – deleted and, where possible, revoked
TikTok account identifier and basic profile data received at authorizationYes
Google Drive file and folder identifiers and video metadataYes
Captions, hashtags and publishing schedulesYes
Publishing history and status logsYes, subject to any legal retention duty [Confirm]

Some data is outside our control and is not deleted by us: videos already published to TikTok (delete these in TikTok), your original files in Google Drive (managed by you), and data held by TikTok, Google or Vercel under their own policies. Where we must keep limited information to comply with law, resolve disputes or prevent abuse, we will tell you what and why.

4. How to contact support

Email your-email@example.com. More details are on our Contact page.

5. Expected deletion process

  1. Acknowledgement: we aim to acknowledge your request within [3 business days].
  2. Verification: we may ask you to confirm ownership of the account.
  3. Revocation and deletion: stored tokens are deleted or revoked, and the data listed above is removed from the systems we control. We aim to complete this within [30 days] of verification.
  4. Confirmation: we will email you when deletion is complete.

Copies in routine backups, if any, are removed on the normal backup rotation cycle [Confirm or remove].

See also our Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
