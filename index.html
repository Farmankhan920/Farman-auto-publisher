Publishing workflow tool

From Google Drive to TikTok, organized and authorized.

Farman Auto Publisher helps a creator prepare videos in Google Drive and publish them to their own TikTok account, using TikTok Login Kit for authorization and the TikTok Content Posting API for publishing.

Features

Designed for a calm, controlled publishing routine

The capabilities below describe what the application is designed to do. Each depends on your authorization and on TikTok's platform rules.

Google Drive video source

Keep your videos in a Google Drive folder you control. Access is limited to the files and folders you choose to use with the workflow.

TikTok Login Kit authorization

You sign in on TikTok's own authorization screen and approve exactly the permissions requested. You can revoke access at any time.

Captions and hashtags

Prepare captions and hashtags for each video before it is submitted, so every post matches what you intended to publish.

Publishing schedules

Plan when videos should be submitted. Scheduling is handled by Google Apps Script automation that runs under your own Google account.

Privacy-minded by design

We aim to collect only what the workflow needs. Our Privacy Policy explains what is handled and why.

Disconnect and delete

You can disconnect your TikTok account and request deletion of stored data at any time. See Data Deletion.

Workflow

Google Drive to TikTok publishing workflow

A simple four-stage flow. You stay in control of which videos are published and when.

Prepare in Drive

Place the videos you own, or have permission to publish, in a Google Drive folder.

Authorize TikTok

Connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and approve the requested permissions.

Set details

Add the caption, hashtags and intended schedule for each video.

Publish via API

At the scheduled time, Google Apps Script requests the post through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Posting behavior, visibility options and limits are determined by TikTok and may change. Unaudited TikTok apps may be restricted by TikTok to private visibility until approved.
