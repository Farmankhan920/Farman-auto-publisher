2. TikTok OAuth authentication

The Service is designed to use TikTok Login Kit (OAuth). You sign in on TikTok's own pages. We do not see or store your TikTok password. TikTok shows you the permissions (scopes) requested, and the Service receives access only to the scopes you approve, for example permission to post content to your account.

Information that TikTok provides to the Service through authorization is used only to identify your connected account and to publish content you direct. TikTok's handling of your data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Access tokens and refresh tokens

After you authorize, TikTok issues an access token (short-lived) and a refresh token (longer-lived) that allow the Service to act within your approved permissions without asking for your password. Tokens are treated as sensitive credentials.

Access tokenAuthorizes API requests to TikTok[e.g., Google Apps Script Properties Service in your own Google account / other]

Tokens are not intentionally shared with anyone except TikTok, are used only to carry out your requested actions, and are deleted or invalidated when you disconnect your account or request deletion, as described below.
